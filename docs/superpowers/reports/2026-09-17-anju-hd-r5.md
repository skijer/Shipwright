# HD Anju R5 review draft

R4's numerical clearance checks passed, but the grip looked detached, the thumb pointed along the shaft, and its replacement elbow changed the arm silhouette too much. R5 corrects those regressions with a narrower geometry pass. No runtime changes or push.

## Changes

- Restore the left arm and sleeve resources byte-for-byte from R3. Remove the R4 elbow loft and its standing corrective roots. The original R3 elbow crease/overlap is retained; this revision does not claim intersection-free elbow topology.
- Fold the thumb across the grip and curl the other fingers independently. Preserve the forearm-derived R4 wrist alignment. Fingernails follow their fingers; no nail faces are trimmed.
- Fit the umbrella cross-section to the hand, rotating accessory normals with its geometry. Keep the hook extension below the palm.
- Trim only skin buried inside the shaft's triangular prism instead of pushing the hand outward. This is a prop-specific contact mesh: the hand and umbrella must remain attached to joint 7 together.
- Preserve the R4 ankle taper and all seven approved texture payloads. Keep the R3 vest, skirt, shoes and relaxed standing left-hand candidate.
- Export separate standing right-arm/hand and umbrella candidates. The default roots remain seated. Standing is an offline candidate with no catalogue parameter.

## Verification

- Actual exported archive decoded through 32 standing and 43 seated native frames: finite positions/normals and zero detected shaft penetrations with a 1-model-unit interior tolerance.
- Closest vertex contact gap: 0.08463 model units standing; 0.16406 seated. Hook clearance exceeds 60 model units in both poses. These measurements complement the front/palm/back visual previews; numerical clearance alone did not establish a natural grip in R4.
- The folded thumbnail's axial center is -73.43 standing and -73.61 seated, compared with the upright R4 position around -288. All 27 source thumbnail faces remain present.
- Existing R3 payload changes are limited to metadata, the right wrist/hand/nails, contact triangles, the left ankle, and umbrella vertices. All left-arm and sleeve payloads are exact R3. Wrist and ankle vertices are exact R4; all seven textures are exact R3.
- Native production graph fixture: PASS, 19 limbs, 3 blink heads, umbrella, 2946 owned vertex loads, complete fallback and 8 Alt/cache-reentry cycles.
- Both GIFs contain every native frame at 50 ms per frame; no duplicate rendered frames. Full-pose and three-angle grip previews were rendered from the exported archive, not a separate illustration.
- Python compilation and `git diff --check` pass. No new in-game result is claimed. Existing anchor, blink and resource-lifetime behavior is untouched.

## Reproduce

Run `build_anju_hd.py` with the native MM archive, original supplied HD Anju archive, four approved hair/clothes/shirt/umbrella PNGs, and `--revision R5 --export-only`. Run `render_anju_hd_fit_review.py` against that exported archive. `verify_anju_hd_contact.py` takes native, original source, R3, R4, R5 and JSON output paths in that order. Earlier revisions remain explicitly selectable.
