# HD Anju, outfit and umbrella draft

Prepared on `feat/anju-umbrella-kafei`, after local baseline `4b87ffd`.
This draft requires its adapter update; the O2R is not intended for an older build.
Preview approval and an in-game test remain outstanding. Nothing is pushed by this
work step.

## Input and appearance

`HD Anju.o2r` contains the HD geometry and its original atlas resources. Its SHA-256
is `facfd2601081ff66fe15bce7c1e2523ee3d9854ca05c66344ef17d33614ba101`.
`HD Anju(1).o2r` contains only scene/path/collision edits and is not a model source.
The exported pack includes no scene, collision, path, skeleton or animation
overrides. Native MM animation and umbrella geometry come from the supplied
`mm(1).o2r`.

- Hair: the approved auburn artwork retains strand highlights and soft sheen.
  The native CI8 hair texture is 8×16; its palette is `gAnju1TLUT`, confirmed from
  the native material. Native mean RGB is `#5C1509`, with a representative colored
  midtone of `#7B0000` after excluding pure black. The approved HD texture's mean
  is `#601308` and middle-luminance band is `#5C0F05`. These are texture statistics,
  not promises about screen pixels under different scene lighting. Luminance
  correlation to the earlier shiny draft is 0.9506, retaining highlight placement.
- The orange hair blotches reproduce offline with the supplied geometry. The
  hair shell has overlapping/two-sided faces, including 50 exactly duplicated
  triangles. Backface culling on that shell removes the visible overlap artifacts.
  Separate eyebrow islands remain two-sided and use the approved hair palette.
- The source blue eyes and face atlas remain. Three head display lists use mesh
  eyelids, clipped iris geometry for the half state, and complete closure for the
  closed state. Upper-lid UVs sample clean skin on the same atlas instead of
  stretching its painted eye crease. This implements blinking through complete
  head states; it does not invent a new set of painted eye textures.
- The supplied shoes gain a narrower heel and raised instep. The ankle seam and
  original sole-contact height remain. Normals are recalculated around edited
  geometry. This is a first fitted heel shape, not a replacement shoe model.
- Clothing keeps its dark teal, brown and pale green palette. Separate refined
  garment artwork adds fabric detail and folds. Only shirt polygons sample the
  shirt refinement; skin and face polygons retain the source atlas. Three small
  inner surfaces above the hem use matching fabric lining to hide skin slivers
  at the bent knees; exposed calves and feet remain unchanged.
- Umbrella shape, size, right-hand attachment and brown/gold material colors stay
  native. Its 32×32 canopy pattern has a sharper HD replacement. The separate
  16×16 circle texture is retained. Logical texture dimensions and I8 material
  behavior are preserved while the private resources carry RGBA32 pixels.

## Runtime contract

The pack uses `objects/object_anju_hd/v1/` and Alternate Assets. All 19 drawn limb
roots, three blink heads and the umbrella must resolve in one archive. Missing,
malformed or out-of-bounds resources select the complete native model. Private
graphs retain lists, vertices and texture aliases across resource-cache eviction
and are keyed by archive identity. Native fallback uses the same owned graph.
Anju's cache is separate from the existing Skull Kid graph cache.

Every Anju instance receives a private native hierarchy with all 20 child resources
retained. Global alternate limbs cannot change those joint offsets. The supplied
mesh's local fit is +476 Y on one-based limbs 2, 10 and 17, applied only in the draw
callback when the complete HD pack is selected. It does not alter actor/home XYZ,
yaw, collision, animation samples or the native fallback.

Skeleton, animation, child and native face resources are parsed with explicit
native binary-header metadata and archive identity. This also bypasses global
metadata redirection if a provider explicitly exposes it. Normal O2R indexing in
this revision strips `.meta` filenames, so this is defensive isolation, not a
reproduced failure caused by the user's supplied archive.

The blink sequence is open → half → closed → closed → half → open at the native
20 Hz update rate, holding fully shut for 100 ms. Idle delay uses the catalogue's
random timer. Timing is per actor and updated outside rendering; live Alt changes
select the whole model again before draw. The native crying face stays sad.

## Build and readback

Python dependencies: NumPy, SciPy and Pillow. Refined PNG artwork is supplied
separately; the converter does not generate or recolor imagery.

```sh
python3 scripts/diagnostics/build_anju_hd.py MM_ARCHIVE SOURCE_HD_ARCHIVE OUTPUT_DIR \
  --hair ART/hair_vanilla_auburn.png --clothes ART/clothes_refined.png \
  --shirt ART/shirt_refined.png --umbrella ART/umbrella_pattern_hd.png --export-only
python3 scripts/diagnostics/render_anju_hd_review.py MM_ARCHIVE \
  OUTPUT_DIR/Anju_HD_Auburn_Umbrella_R1.o2r PREVIEW_DIR
```

The converter validates vertex and matrix bounds and its ZIP CRC before writing.
The preview reads the exported display lists and textures, applies all 43 native
animation frames, and renders at 20 fps. Separate head previews read the three
exported states under a neutral transform. These are offline CPU renders, not
game captures or validation of scene lighting.

- The production-function viewer fixture checks all limbs, head and umbrella
  selection, live Alt changes, 100 ms closure, independent instances, extra draws,
  bounded invalid phases and preserved actor Y/yaw through 86 updates per scene.

  All 20 native children and their offsets survive four cache-clear cycles.

  loader capability. The ordinary helper demonstrably follows its one-frame
  animation redirect, while Anju retains its 43-frame native clip, exact offsets
  and native resource ownership with Alt off/on.
- The actual model graph fixture verifies all three distinct heads, 19 limbs,
  umbrella, 3,014 owned vertex loads and resource provenance. Missing closed head,
